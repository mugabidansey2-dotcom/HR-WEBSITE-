About this system

The problem

Many small offices calculate salaries by hand. Mistakes in allowances or tax deductions lead to wrong pay and extra rework each month.

Who it is for

HR officers, accountants and administrators who prepare monthly payroll.

Objectives

  • Calculate gross pay and net pay accurately.
  • Show a clear payslip for each employee.
  • Produce a payroll summary with totals.
  • Keep a consistent, easy-to-use layout on desktop and mobile.

Built with

HTML for structure, CSS for styling and responsiveness, and JavaScript for calculation, validation, search and the mobile menu.
